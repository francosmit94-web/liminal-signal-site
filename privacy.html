Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This policy explains how Liminal Signal ("we") handles data in connection with the Liminal Signal Uploader application ("the app") and our website.

Who uses the app

The app is an internal tool. The only accounts it connects to are Liminal Signal's own channels. It does not offer sign-in to the public.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube (via YouTube API Services): with the permissions granted by the channel owner, the app uploads videos to Liminal Signal's YouTube channel (scope youtube.upload) and reads that channel's basic information and its videos' public statistics such as views, likes and comments (scope youtube.readonly).
  • OAuth tokens: the access and refresh tokens Google issues for our channel. They are stored encrypted on infrastructure we control and used only to perform the actions above.

How the data is used

Only to publish our own videos and to measure how they perform. We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone, do not use it for advertising, and do not combine it with other data about individuals. We do not collect personal data about viewers.

Google API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, our channel is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Video statistics are kept for as long as they are useful for our reporting. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings; revoking it stops all access immediately, and we delete the stored tokens. To ask for deletion of any data, email us.

Other platforms

We also publish to other platforms (such as TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads and Bluesky) using their official APIs in the same way: only to post our own videos to our own accounts and read their statistics.
